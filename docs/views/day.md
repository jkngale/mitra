title: Day view

  label: Day
---

The day view focuses on one date. It uses the week view's hour-by-hour grid, but gives the selected day the full width so you can see its schedule and plan around the time you have left. Open it from the view picker or press <kbd>D</kbd>.

Use the arrows beside **Today** to move one day at a time. <kbd>T</kbd> returns to today, and <kbd>G</kbd> jumps to a date. The view follows the date you select elsewhere in Mitra.

All-day entries appear above the time grid; timed events and tasks appear in the schedule. Drag across an empty time to create an entry, or drag an entry to move or resize it. The day view supports the same time-zone lanes, availability and dependency links as the week view.
