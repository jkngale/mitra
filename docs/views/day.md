The day view shows one date across the full width, on the same hour-by-hour grid as the [week](week.md), so a busy day has room to read. Open it from the view picker or press <kbd>D</kbd>.

The arrows beside **Today** move one day at a time, and scrolling or swiping sideways moves to the days around it. <kbd>T</kbd> returns to today, and <kbd>G</kbd> jumps to a date.

Everything the week view does works here too: drag across an empty time to create an entry, drag an entry to move or resize it, and see all-day entries above the grid, time zones, availability and dependencies.
